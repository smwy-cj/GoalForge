# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Browse news and general information on the social network web application daily.
  适用条件：Daily, habitual use of the application.
- Search for job posting information on the application.
  适用条件：Occasional ('sometimes'), not the primary daily use.
- Avoid missing important information such as major international events and new job postings.
  适用条件：Examples given: major international events and new job postings.
- Receive continuously updated AI recommendations of the latest content that matches the user's taste, based on recorded likes and comments.
  适用条件：Described as 'the necessary feature for social applications'.；Recommendations draw on recorded likes, comments and browsing history.
- Scroll up and down the main-page feed to locate intended postings.
  适用条件：One of the two main actions the user reports for information browsing.
- Open the daily news ranking and select the specific news item wanted.
  适用条件：The other main action the user reports for information browsing.
- Search for information and obtain matching results.
  适用条件：Performed through the search bar at the top of the application; the user is unsure whether scrolling counts as a feature.
- Search information and results restricted to a specific region such as a city, through a dedicated button or search condition.
  适用条件：Stated as a preference ('it would be better for me'), not a mandatory requirement.；Motivating case: searching house rentals in a specific city; without region narrowing many useful postings are filtered out.
- See how many people are already in the hiring process for a company, in order to judge whether the company is hiring in large numbers.
  适用条件：Expressed tentatively ('I might want to see').
- Get feedback from recent interviewees about a company, in order to judge how high its interview benchmark is.
  适用条件：Purpose: judge whether the company has a high interview benchmark.
- Have a feature that separates entertainment content from useful content and filters entertainment out when the user does not want to see it.
  适用条件：Stated as a preference ('it would be better'), not a mandatory requirement.；Applies when the user does not want to see entertainment content; mentions job searching and housing rental searching.
- Receive light entertainment content such as daily jokes pushed by the application.
  适用条件：Expressed tentatively ('maybe'); an additional use alongside information browsing.
- Improve the platform's data management so that user data is not sold to other parties.
  适用条件：Expressed tentatively ('they might just'); directed at the company rather than a confirmed commitment.
- Strengthen handling of fake or matrix accounts that post identical marketing content and flood the feed with irrelevant content.
  适用条件：The user frames this as an improvement to be done by the development team.
