# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Browse the application's news and general information feed daily to keep up with daily information.
  适用条件：每日使用；原文报告的现有入口为主页信息流滚动与每日新闻排行（T007）
- Avoid missing important information such as major international events and new job postings.
  适用条件：重要信息举例含国际事件与新岗位；同一轮提到难以从社交应用拿到第一手信息，转写含糊（T003），未据此另建目标
- Search the application for job postings.
  适用条件：与信息浏览并列的另一个日常用途（T001、T016）
- Narrow content search results to a specific region such as a city, so that region-specific items like housing rentals can be found.
  适用条件：以租房信息为例；受访者确认搜索应增加限制/条件（T014—T015）；建议形态为专门按钮或类似功能，方式未唯一确定（T013）；岗位搜索因已有 AI 推荐被认为问题较少（T017）
- See how many people are already in a company's hiring process, in order to judge whether the company is hiring in large numbers.
  适用条件：求职时希望从应用获得的信息（T018 提问、T019 回答）；语气为 might want
- Read feedback from people who recently interviewed at a company, in order to judge how demanding its interview process is.
  适用条件：同上，属求职时希望看到的信息（T018—T019）
- Filter entertainment content out of the results when the user does not want to see it, so that useful job and housing content is kept separate.
  适用条件：适用于求职、租房等实用内容检索时；同一受访者仍会在其他时候消费娱乐内容（T025），故限定为“不想看时”
- Receive continuously pushed latest information that matches the taste inferred from the user's likes, comments and browsing history.
  适用条件：受访者称这是社交应用的必要功能（T005）；基于点赞/评论记录与浏览历史的推断（T005、T029）
- Ensure personal data is not sold to third parties by improving the platform's data management.
  适用条件：弱建议：回答“公司应如何解决”时用 might just improve their management；受访者同时对已发生泄露表示接受、不太在意（T033、T037），本目标强度不因此提高
- Strengthen handling of matrix marketing accounts that post duplicated content and flood the feed with irrelevant content.
  适用条件：理由是重复营销内容淹没信息流、干扰用户体验（T043）；受访者称这是开发团队应做的改进之一
- View light entertainment content such as daily jokes that the application pushes.
  适用条件：语气为 maybe，属信息浏览之外的附带用途；原文报告现有形式为图文（T027）
