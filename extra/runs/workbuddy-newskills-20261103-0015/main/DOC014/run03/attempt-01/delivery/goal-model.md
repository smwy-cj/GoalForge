# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Check the outside temperature for the next day, usually the day before, including the morning (before-class / when-going-out) hourly values.
  适用条件：日常例行；通常提前一天看次日；打开时首先看温度与降雨计之一；出门前/上课前早晨时段的每小时温度
- Check the rain / precipitation chances for tomorrow (the next day), including the hourly percentage where 25+ means a good chance of rain.
  适用条件：日常例行；打开时与温度并列首先查看；1-100 刻度，25+ 表示降雨可能较大
- Plan whether to wear the thick coat or the light coat based on the checked temperature.
  适用条件：日常；依据所查温度
- When it is super cold, skip cycling and take the bus instead (to avoid cycling down in the cold).
  适用条件：仅在 super cold 时；语气为 might，非强制
- Make sure to have an umbrella when rain is forecast.
  适用条件：当有降雨预报时
- Learn about an important/severe weather event first from weather.com when it has not already been seen in the news, including warnings and definite notice when it happens for my city.
  适用条件：极端天气事件场景；前提是尚未从新闻得知；语气 would be a good spot / definitely
- Have/consult a safety guide or resources from weather.com during an extreme weather event (so as to stay safe).
  适用条件：极端天气事件场景；仅为受访者假设，自述从未见过指南/未经历过
- On a typhoon alert, open the notification to check the severity and all details about the typhoon.
  适用条件：收到台风警报时；语气 probably
- Read what the authorities recommend (whether to evacuate the city, seek shelter / not go outside, or just carry an umbrella and a raincoat).
  适用条件：天气警报场景；建议来自 local authorities / Government meteorological organization
- Adjust my day based on the severity and the information given.
  适用条件：天气警报场景；取决于 severity 与所获信息
- Read important weather events happening across the world (not necessarily in my city) via the main-page sections / news tab, including the event details.
  适用条件：主页板块/新闻标签；点击后打开 news tab
- When a trip is known (a couple of weeks ahead), check historical weather data if available, or the future/monthly forecast, to get an estimate of what the weather will be like.
  适用条件：确定要出行后立即/提前数周；历史数据仅在 if it's available somewhere 时；或选未来/月度预报
- Check cloud coverage (in addition to temperature and precipitation) when planning a trip, which may lead to reconsidering or cancelling a beach vacation or astronomical-event trip.
  适用条件：旅行规划；海滩/天文观测为举例情境；结果语气 might need to reconsider / might have to cancel
- Wait till a couple of days (2-3 days) before the trip and decide based on the final, more accurate forecast, since a few-weeks-out prediction is hard and can be the difference between going and not going.
  适用条件：旅行决策；几周前难准；临近 2-3 天通常较准；语气 should probably
- View more precise hourly weather data (including wind speed, humidity, UV index and what it actually feels like) via the hourly tab when wanting more detail.
  适用条件：仅在 if you want more precise hourly coverage 时；通过 hourly tab / 点击某一小时
- Screenshot and share on social media a weather reading showing the temperature below 0 (planned, not yet done).
  适用条件：当看到温度显示低于 0（家乡从不低于 0）；I'm planning to / I'll probably / haven't done it yet；不一定直接从 app 分享
- Have a plain black or plain white background instead of the dull blue color scheme (preferred).
  适用条件：UI 配色；语气 I would prefer that，非强制
- Reduce the number of ads (4-5 ads plus video pop-up ads are too much; 1-2 ads would be acceptable) so they do not distract from quickly checking the weather.
  适用条件：抱怨 4-5 个广告加视频弹窗 too much / annoying；可接受 1-2 个广告；影响快速查看完就离开的体验
- Make the UI more compact (squish it / bring elements closer together) so it is easier to read, instead of having content separated by big ad chunks that require scrolling up and down.
  适用条件：改进建议 Maybe they can；definitely on top on the list（最优先）
- Improve the radar section to be more detailed and intuitive, with multiple parameters (e.g. snow coverage, precipitation), like the windy.com radar.
  适用条件：改善愿望 could be better；参照某创业公司/windy.com 雷达；若做得好 might consider buying
- Provide very local weather data for non-city locations (e.g. a hotel in the middle of nowhere, hiking trails, mountain-climbing expeditions) where city-level weather does not help.
  适用条件：由采访者给出徒步/偏僻地点场景后受访者认可；语气 would be a good feature / definitely helpful；T065 曾表示想不出任何新增功能、已很全面，构成相反证据
- Keep notifications from weather.com turned off (prefer no notifications; if turned on it is only a next-day weather highlight).
  适用条件：Mac/PC (Chrome)；语气 prefer / I've kept it off；若开启仅次日天气要点
- View temperatures in Celsius (preferred) instead of the default Fahrenheit, using the C/F drop down at the top.
  适用条件：偏好 I do prefer，非强制；默认华氏；顶部下拉可切换
- Keep/view weather for the two saved cities (the home city and the city coming from in India).
  适用条件：已设置并保持打开 (I have it open)；除城市与度数外无其他个性化设置
- Avoid location/city data (and email-linked cities) being exposed in a leak when logged in.
  适用条件：仅在登录且发生泄露的假设下；数据仅存本地缓存/不被存储均为受访者猜测 (not 100% sure)；受访者明确说 otherwise not too worried
