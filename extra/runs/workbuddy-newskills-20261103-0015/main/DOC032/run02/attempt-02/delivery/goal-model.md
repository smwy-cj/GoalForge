# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Communicate with his friends and family through WeChat as his primary IM app.
  适用条件：交流经由微信进行，因为周围人都在使用微信（T007 网络效应）
- Send a message to a colleague by locating him through the search bar at the top of the screen, opening a private chat or group chat, and then typing and sending the message.
- Organize and manage his chat list (chat box) in WeChat.
- Pin the chats he cares most about (e.g., the chat with his girlfriend, certain groups) to the top of the chat list so they are the first chat boxes he sees whenever he opens WeChat.
- Find specific messages by typing keywords into the search bar at the top of the screen, retrieving all relevant messages from both group chats and private chats.
- Search for messages within a specific chat, limited to those just between him and that contact, using the chat's additional settings search.
- Retrieve a previously sent image within a chat by searching with a text keyword (e.g., credit card), instead of scrolling back through the chat history.
- Pay and transfer money to friends or merchants (e.g., a restaurant) via WeChat's payment system, either by scanning the counterparty's QR code or by showing his own QR code for them to scan.
  适用条件：可绑定信用卡或借记卡（T033）；确认转账时以面容 ID 或指纹等验证（T037）
- Send files to friends; when a file exceeds WeChat's transfer size limit (about 25 MB), upload it to a cloud drive and send the download link instead.
  适用条件：微信限制可传输文件大小约为 25MB（T055）
- Keep all his chat messages when changing to a new phone.
  适用条件：微信出于隐私或存储成本不在云端存储消息，全部消息在手机本地（T059）；目前只能手动把全部消息转移到新手机，非常耗时（T059，T061 确认）
- Avoid permanently losing all his chat messages if his phone is lost.
- Have WeChat support cloud storage for all his messages so they remain available at all times and no longer occupy his iPhone storage.
  适用条件：云端存储实现后，换手机时无需再担心消息（T071）

## 目标之间的联系

下列联系不表示充分精化；待确认及已否定记录保留各自状态。

- 有依据的联系：Pin the chats he cares most about (e.g., the chat with his girlfriend, certain groups) to the top of the chat list so they are the first chat boxes he sees whenever he opens WeChat. —[有助于]→ Communicate with his friends and family through WeChat as his primary IM app.
  依据：置顶使最关心的亲友会话在他打开微信时最先可见，直接降低与这些亲友交流的触达成本，有助于实现与朋友和家人交流的主要目标；引文同时表明置顶对象正是亲友，效果是打开应用首先可见。
- 有依据的联系：Retrieve a previously sent image within a chat by searching with a text keyword (e.g., credit card), instead of scrolling back through the chat history. —[范围具体化]→ Search for messages within a specific chat, limited to those just between him and that contact, using the chat's additional settings search.
  依据：按文本关键词找图片是会话内消息搜索的具体情境：T027 以 even 表明图片检索是会话内搜索能力的延伸，T045 显示他进入具体聊天界面输入关键词检索图片；具体情境目标（图片检索）指向一般目标（会话内限双方消息搜索）。
- 有依据的联系：Have WeChat support cloud storage for all his messages so they remain available at all times and no longer occupy his iPhone storage. —[有助于]→ Keep all his chat messages when changing to a new phone.
  依据：T071 明确说明若微信支持消息云端存储，换手机时就无需再担心；云端存储（保留全部消息随时可用）直接有助于换机时保留全部消息的目标。
