# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Find a new place to live when moving to a new neighborhood or a new country.
  适用条件：Trigger: relocation to a new neighbourhood or another country
- Choose the neighborhood or area to search in before starting the keyword search for that area.
  适用条件：The keyword search then targets the chosen area
- Identify which rental websites are the most popular or most used in the target area, especially when it is another country.
  适用条件：The area may be in another country
- Pick a neighborhood close to a fixed destination such as a job or a college.
  适用条件：Only when a job or a college anchors the location
- Ask people who live in the target country for recommendations about places.
  适用条件：One of several ways to choose a neighbourhood, not the only method
- Spot the neighborhoods where listings that fit the budget and requirements are clustered.
  适用条件：Based on sites showing available houses and apartments
- Search listings by primary criteria: price range, size or bedroom and bathroom counts, and home type.
  适用条件：Size or bedroom count is paired with a budget limit；Home type distinguishes a house from an apartment
- Narrow the results down with secondary criteria such as amenities, utilities, pet policy, move-in date and how good the place looks.
  适用条件：Applied after the primary search returns many options；These criteria are described as less important than size and price
- Contact the people offering the shortlisted places to narrow the options down, letting reply speed, completeness of information and provider attitude affect the choice.
  适用条件：After filtering down to several options；A provider attitude that raises suspicion counts against the option
- Get an overview of the available options and price ranges, including reviews and locations, when looking in a new market.
  适用条件：Especially when moving to another country；Information the stakeholder could not obtain without such websites
- Reach a provider through their own website, email or phone found via the place name and Google Maps when the in-app contact gets no response.
  适用条件：Triggered when the provider does not answer through the platform
- Use listing platforms mainly to discover places and carry the communication on outside the apps.
  适用条件：Current practice, described as usual
- See when a listing's information was last updated so that I can be assured it is current.
  适用条件：Because the information may be outdated or wrong
- See a timeline of the next steps and of what to expect after an inquiry, including what happens if it fails.
  适用条件：Would make the stakeholder more comfortable and more willing to engage
- Contact a provider with a short message instead of a long mandatory form and without handing over all personal details up front.
  适用条件：The current form requires first name, last name, email, mobile, move dates, beds, baths and a custom message
- Share personal details only after both sides confirm availability and interest.
  适用条件：Before that confirmation the information is not considered necessary
- Pick a specific available unit from the listed options instead of writing a custom message.
  适用条件：Housing options are limited and not customised, unlike made-to-order goods
- Search and use the full functionality without being required to register or log in, keeping registration optional for saving information.
  适用条件：Registration is acceptable only as an option to save details and autofill
- Search the information I want conveniently, without steps I do not consider beneficial.
  适用条件：Stated as the primary goal behind skipping login
- Keep the interface to few, clearly understood actions and avoid unknown buttons or extra steps.
  适用条件：Unknown extra steps make the stakeholder leave the site
- Select the search area by drawing a circle on a map instead of relying only on neighborhood names.
  适用条件：Neighborhood-name search is too specific and misses nearby options
- Read reviews from people about a place so that it feels more trusted and the information sits together.
- Compare three or four liked options on a single page with their main features side by side.
  适用条件：The stakeholder has not found a website that offers this
- Save interesting options to a favorites list that can be found from the nav bar or the first level of the menu.
  适用条件：Currently the saved items cannot be located after saving
- Avoid losing suitable options whose attributes the provider left unspecified when filtering.
  适用条件：Example: an owner may allow pets without stating it in the profile
- Keep the filters optional rather than forcing the user to fill every field.
  适用条件：Sometimes the stakeholder is not sure what he wants
- Include listings that did not specify a criterion when the user asks for it, for example through a checkbox, so that both confirmed and unspecified options are shown.
  适用条件：Without the checkbox only listings that confirm the criterion are shown
- Have the website enforce a standard photo format (light and angles) for listings so that the photos are more trustworthy.
  适用条件：Proposed tentatively ('maybe'); expected effect is more trust in the site
- Keep all housing communication with property owners inside the website, provided the process is clear and fast.
  适用条件：Condition: the communication process is clear and fast；The stakeholder wants to decide within a week or at most a month
