# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Find a new place to live when moving to a new neighborhood or country, by searching on the internet with house-hunting web apps.
  适用条件：moving to a new neighborhood or a new country
- Choose the neighborhood or area to search in before starting a house search.
- Identify the listing websites that are most popular or most used in the target area, especially when the area is in another country.
  适用条件：especially when the target area is in another country
- Find neighborhoods where listings that fit my budget and requirements are clustered.
- Get recommendations on places from people who live in the target country.
  适用条件：the target country is not where I live
- Pick a neighborhood close to my job or college when I have one.
  适用条件：when I have a specific job or study at a specific college
- Search listings by size or bedroom count within a budget limit.
- Filter the retrieved options down by secondary criteria such as amenities, extra features, or how good the place looks.
  适用条件：after the first search returns many options
- Contact the providers of the shortlisted places and narrow the options by how fast and how completely they respond.
  适用条件：after filtering down to several options
- Reach a provider who does not answer through the app by finding the place's name, location or website and contacting them by email or phone.
  适用条件：the provider does not respond through the app
- Keep the conversation with providers outside the listing apps, using the apps only to discover places.
  适用条件：based on reported practice and distrust of in-app messaging
- See when the listing information was last updated.
- See a timeline of the next steps and what to expect after sending a request, including what happens if it fails.
  适用条件：after a request is sent
- Contact a provider with minimal information instead of filling a long form in which every field is required.
  适用条件：current form requires first name, last name, email, mobile, move end date, beds, baths and a custom message
- Share my personal information only after both sides have confirmed availability and interest.
  适用条件：before confirmation it is not necessary to give all the information
- See the few matching units listed with their specifications and add the chosen one to a shortlist, e-commerce style.
  适用条件：when only a few units match the specifications
- Compare three or four shortlisted options on a single page by their main features.
  适用条件：three or four options I like
- Find my saved options from the nav bar or the first level of the menu.
- Search inside an area drawn on the map instead of only by neighborhood name.
  适用条件：when searching by neighborhood name is too narrow
- See reviews from people who lived in the place so that listings feel more trusted.
- Avoid extra unknown buttons and steps whose outcome I cannot tell when searching on a site.
- Search and use the full functionality without registering, with an account optional.
- Filter listings by price range, beds and baths, home type, move-in date, pets or utilities, without being forced to fill every filter.
  适用条件：filters stay optional
- Also see, when a filter is applied, the listings whose owner did not specify that attribute.
  适用条件：a filter on an attribute such as pet policy is applied
- Get an overview of the price ranges and available options in an area I do not know yet.
  适用条件：moving to another country
- Have the site require owners to photograph listings in a standard format (sunlight, specific angles) so that pictures are more trustworthy.
  适用条件：stated as a suggestion (maybe), not a firm requirement
- Keep all housing communication on the listing platform when the process is clear and fast.
  适用条件：only if the process is clear and fast；decision window is about a week, at most a month

- 照片格式规则只是以 maybe 提出的建议，是否应作为对平台的要求保留？
- “流程清晰且快速”没有可核对的时限或状态标准，是否需要补充定义？
- 精化组判断（已执行）：G004、G005、G006 被原文描述为选街区的多种方式（“there are many ways”），属 OR 替代而非共同满足，因此未建 AND 组；是否需要以多个 OR 方案表达，请确认。
- 精化组判断（已执行）：曾评估以 G002（选址）、G007（按面积/预算搜索）、G008（次要条件筛选）共同满足 G001，但组内缺少联系房东与核验真实性，无法声称共同充分，故未建组。
