# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Find a new place to live when moving to a new neighborhood or a new country, using housing web apps as the usual way to search.
  适用条件：moving to a new neighborhood or to a new country
- Select the neighborhood or area in which to look for a house.
- Check housing websites to see in which neighborhoods the listings that fit my budget and requirements are clustered, as one way to choose the area.
  适用条件：as one of the ways to choose the neighborhood
- Ask people who live in the target country for recommendations of places to look at.
  适用条件：as another way to choose the neighborhood
- Pick a neighborhood close to the place I regularly go to, such as a specific job or college.
  适用条件：when the daily destination is a specific job or college
- Identify which housing websites are the most popular or most used in the target area by searching with location keywords.
  适用条件：especially when the search is in another country
- Draw an area on the map to define the search area, instead of searching only by neighborhood name.
  适用条件：when the neighborhood name is too narrow and a nearby option could be missed
- Filter the listings by price range, size or bedroom count and home type, and by extra criteria such as move-in date, pets and utilities.
  适用条件：as the first filtering pass, before the lower-priority criteria
- Narrow the remaining options further with lower-priority criteria such as amenities, extra features and how good the place looks.
  适用条件：after the primary filtering, when many options remain
- Contact the people offering the shortlisted places after the options have been narrowed down.
  适用条件：after the options are narrowed down to several
- Narrow the options further using how fast and how completely the providers reply.
  适用条件：while communicating with the providers of the shortlisted options
- Contact the provider through other channels such as their own website, phone or email when they do not respond in the app.
  适用条件：when the provider does not reply through the platform
- Carry out the communication with providers outside the housing apps, keeping those apps mainly as a way to discover places.
- See when the listing information was last updated, so that I can be assured it is up to date.
  适用条件：on the listing details page
- See what happens after contacting a provider, including whether and when they will reply and what to expect if the contact fails.
  适用条件：after contacting a provider, including when the contact fails
- Communicate with the provider in a short, direct way instead of through a long form whose submission I cannot follow.
- See reviews from people who have lived there, so that the place feels more trusted and the information is in one place.
  适用条件：on the listing details page, when reviews from previous residents exist
- Have the website enforce a standard photo format for listings, so that listing photos can be trusted.
  适用条件：subject to the website being able to enforce such rules on owners
- Contact a provider without having to fill in all the mandatory personal fields, the exact bed and bath counts and a custom message.
  适用条件：the current form requires first name, last name, email, mobile number, move end date, number of beds, number of baths and a custom message
- Share my personal information only after both sides have confirmed availability and interest.
  适用条件：after both parties confirm availability and interest
- Start searching and use the site's full functionality without registering or logging in.
  适用条件：registration may remain optional for saving details and pre-filling them
- Avoid long processes with buttons whose outcome I cannot predict, preferring sites that keep the options minimal and list what can be done.
- Find the listings I have favorited in the nav bar or at the first level of the menu after saving them.
  适用条件：after saving a listing with the favorites button
- Compare three or four shortlisted options side by side on a single page.
  适用条件：when 3 or 4 options are shortlisted
- Keep the filters optional, so that I am not forced to enter criteria I am not sure about.
  适用条件：filters are not mandatory fields
- Be able to include listings whose provider left a criterion unspecified when filtering, alongside the listings that confirmed it.
  适用条件：for criteria such as pet policy that owners may leave unspecified
- Get an overview of the available options and their price ranges in the new area.
  适用条件：especially when moving to another country
- Carry out all housing communication with providers in one place on the website.
  适用条件：only if the process is clear and fast
- Reach a home decision within a week, or at most a month, in the typical case.
  适用条件：in the typical case, not as a hard deadline
