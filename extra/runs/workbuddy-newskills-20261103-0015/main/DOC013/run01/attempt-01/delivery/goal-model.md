# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Viewer can locate a specific type of video or content by searching with text or by applying filters.
  适用条件：viewer side；text search and filters are alternative means of the same locating action
- Viewer can browse visually through a large volume of content options, seeing thumbnails with descriptions and creator or actor information, and click through them.
  适用条件：viewer side；visual browsing distinct from text search
- Creator can create and upload a video or start a live stream from a creation option in the platform.
  适用条件：creator or uploader side；upload and live stream are stated alternatives
- Creator can attach a description to an uploaded video and, optionally, a transcript or captions.
  适用条件：transcript/captions are hedged with 'perhaps', weaker than the description
- Creator can edit an uploaded video by adding sound effects, trimming and cropping it.
  适用条件：described as 'very useful', particularly for video streaming
- The platform must ensure that some viewers cannot see certain content that is not suitable for all viewers.
  适用条件：content that is not suitable for all viewers
- The platform must not allow streamers to share certain kinds of content with anybody.
  适用条件：certain kinds of content, e.g. content with explicit language
- A search made with vague or imprecise criteria still returns meaningful results to the viewer.
  适用条件：vague search criteria
- Video playback delivers a seamless stream of buffered data that does not freeze constantly on the viewer's machine.
  适用条件：while viewing content on the viewer's machine
- Viewer can obtain the information they are after quickly through search instead of reading long-form material such as a book.
  适用条件：education and entertainment use of the platform
- Uploads accept the creator's existing video formats without compatibility problems.
  适用条件：creator already has a finished video file
- Uploads are not restricted by video length requirements.
  适用条件：video length
- Publishing an uploaded video to the web is a simple process.
- The creator does not have to wait for the whole video to be uploaded before it can be published.
- Player controls are discoverable to first-time users, including while a video is playing.
  适用条件：first-time users；during playback controls are hidden until the mouse moves to the bottom
- Captions are accurate for viewers who do not speak English as a first language.
  适用条件：non-native English speakers
- Player colors can be adjusted for colorblind viewers.
  适用条件：colorblind viewers；raised as 'could be grounds for some work', current capability unknown
- The platform offers a premium tier that gives access to all content without ad interruptions.
  适用条件：paid subscription tier
- Premium subscription offers individual plans paid monthly or annually, a family plan for up to 5 members, and a student plan with a 1-month free trial.
  适用条件：premium tier
- User selects one premium plan through radio buttons and is then prompted for payment.
  适用条件：premium subscription flow
- Viewer can add content to a watch list.
- Viewer can follow or subscribe to creators and see the latest content from subscribed creators in a central subscriptions area.
  适用条件：central subscriptions area reachable from the hamburger menu
- Streaming can only begin 24 hours after a streaming account is set up.
  适用条件：newly set up streaming account；security and compliance requirement

## 目标之间的联系

下列联系不表示充分精化；待确认及已否定记录保留各自状态。

- 有依据的联系：Viewer can locate a specific type of video or content by searching with text or by applying filters. —[实现方式]→ Viewer can obtain the information they are after quickly through search instead of reading long-form material such as a book.
  依据：受访者把'快速搜索拿到所要的信息'作为使用平台的方式，文字搜索/筛选正是这一方式的具体化，故按实现方式→目的建边。
  条件／假设：viewer seeks information on a topic covered by video content；按特定类型定位内容是实现快速获取信息的手段
- 有依据的联系：A search made with vague or imprecise criteria still returns meaningful results to the viewer. —[有助于]→ Viewer can locate a specific type of video or content by searching with text or by applying filters.
  依据：模糊条件仍返回有意义结果是搜索可用性的质量要求，直接帮助'定位特定内容'这一目标，但不保证其充分实现。
  条件／假设：search terms are vague or imprecise；有意义的结果使文字/筛选搜索达到定位目的
- 有依据的联系：Creator can attach a description to an uploaded video and, optionally, a transcript or captions. —[有助于]→ Viewer can browse visually through a large volume of content options, seeing thumbnails with descriptions and creator or actor information, and click through them.
  依据：浏览目标要求缩略图附描述，而描述由创作者在上传时填写，故创作者侧的描述录入帮助观众侧的浏览发现；两者为同一段需求的两端。
  条件／假设：creator supplies the description at publish time；浏览列表展示的是创作者填写的描述
- 待确认联系：Streaming can only begin 24 hours after a streaming account is set up. —[有助于]→ The platform must not allow streamers to share certain kinds of content with anybody.
  依据：受访者把 24 小时等待显式连回'安全与合规'问题，二者主题相连；但原文未说明该等待如何阻止违规内容，机制属分析推断，故标 proposed。
  条件／假设：new streaming account；24 小时等待用于在开播前完成账号/内容合规处理
- 待确认联系：Creator can edit an uploaded video by adding sound effects, trimming and cropping it. —[有助于]→ Creator can create and upload a video or start a live stream from a creation option in the platform.
  依据：剪辑能力与上传同属创作者流程，剪辑可提升上传成品的质量；但原文只说编辑'很有用'，未说明其服务于上传目标，帮助关系属推测，故标 proposed。
  条件／假设：剪辑是上传流程中的可选增强
