# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Receive recruiter emails while applying for jobs without losing any new information from them.
  适用条件：The inbox is checked every day, at least five times a day (ten to twenty times in practice), so as not to miss recruiting messages
- Receive the news and notifications broadcast by the school through email.
- Book dentist or clinic appointments through email.
- Receive merchandise sales and discount information by email.
- Contact friends by email, although only rarely.
  适用条件：Rarely used compared with other purposes
- Delete unimportant emails, and important emails whose conversation has ended, so that the inbox stays clear and manageable.
  适用条件：Deletion is the stakeholder's own strategy for keeping the inbox manageable
- Tag emails that require a reply or a follow-up task, and return to them when available to finish the task.
  适用条件：Used when the reply or task cannot be done immediately
- Keep separate email accounts for recruiting, school notifications and special deals so that important mail stays organized.
  适用条件：Described as one of two strategies for keeping the inbox manageable
- Get a well-organized email composed by AI assistance built into the client from a few listed main points, then adjust the draft before sending.
  适用条件：An external AI assistant tool is used today; the requested feature is AI assistance built into the email client
- Adjust the font size and make the important sentences bold in the composed email before sending it.
- Send multimedia as a cloud link instead of an attachment so that the recipient can view it on any platform, including mobile and laptop.
  适用条件：Attachments with multimedia are unusual for this stakeholder
- Keep important emails out of the spam folder when the provider categorizes mail automatically.
  适用条件：Preference: rather receive some spam than ignore an important message；Applies to automatic categorization by the email provider
- Edit an email after it has been sent instead of sending a correction as a new email.
  适用条件：The recipient sees the new email as well as the old one, and can view the editing history if they want；Currently Gmail only allows cancelling the send within 3 seconds
- Receive every email that is sent to the inbox, including website registration emails that currently never arrive.
  适用条件：Reported as a reliability obstacle, not as an explicitly requested feature
- Warn the user before sending when the email text mentions an attachment that has not actually been attached.
  适用条件：Example given: writing that the resume is attached without attaching it
- Have the AI assistant extract the main point of a received email.
- Highlight the keywords in a received email so that they are not missed.
  适用条件：Illustrated by a small line in a TikTok assessment email that was missed and cost time
- Spend less time reading every email in the inbox.
  适用条件：Identified as the routine task that could be simplified
- Receive notifications for important incoming messages.
  适用条件：Do not rely fully on automatic importance detection unless it is really reliable, because the AI may miss an important message；Only “some” important messages are mentioned
- Forward received emails to other people.
  适用条件：Regarded as very important when working to a deadline
- Send one email to all members of a group or team by BCC so that the message reaches many people within a short time.
  适用条件：Regarded as very important when working to a deadline；BCC was clarified as bulk email to a large number of people
- Find emails through search, which the stakeholder ranks as the most important feature and uses every day.
  适用条件：Ranked first, above scheduling and tag filtering；Tag filtering is regarded as achievable through search
- Schedule emails to be sent at a later time.
  适用条件：Ranked second in importance, after search；The feature was introduced by the interviewer as scheduling mails to be sent at different times
- Support category or protocol email addresses so that two parties who register the same category ID can identify each other's category and communicate.
  适用条件：Presented tentatively; the stakeholder says it is probably not that practical；Example: a tag category for recruiting notifications between the user and HR

- 有依据的联系：Have the AI assistant extract the main point of a received email. —[实现方式]→ Spend less time reading every email in the inbox.
