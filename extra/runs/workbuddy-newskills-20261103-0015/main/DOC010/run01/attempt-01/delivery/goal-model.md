# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Plan what to wear and what to carry on a given day based on the weather.
  适用条件：Checked in the morning as part of the waking routine；Daily use
- Plan outdoor activities with friends based on the weather, buying tickets for them only when the forecast is suitable.
  适用条件：Activities are planned together with friends；Tickets are bought in advance for the activity
- Check the forecast for the entire week at the destination when planning a trip, before deciding on activities and buying tickets.
  适用条件：Trip to a place he has not been to before；Whole-week view used for trips; daily checks otherwise
- Check the weather in a new place to become familiar with its conditions.
  适用条件：Only in a new or unfamiliar place；Not needed in places he has lived in for a long time (exception)
- See extreme weather conditions (thunderstorm, flooding) in the app so as to postpone the planned event and not go out.
  适用条件：Extreme conditions such as thunderstorm or flooding
- Receive notifications or alerts for extreme weather conditions.
  适用条件：Extreme conditions
- Receive routine weather notifications about three times a day (morning, afternoon, evening) aligned with going-out times, instead of constant pop-ups.
  适用条件：Routine, non-extreme conditions；Times matched to when he usually goes out
- Receive frequent updates while rain is ongoing, covering when it starts and stops, and hourly updates for rain and extreme conditions.
  适用条件：Limited to unfavorable weather (rain, extreme conditions); regular weekly or daily updates are enough otherwise
- Provide real-time weather updates so that upcoming changes are reflected when deciding whether to go out.
  适用条件：When deciding whether to go out, e.g., walk or take the bus
- Show no ads in the weather app.
  适用条件：Ads appeared when he had not subscribed to the app
- Keep weather information consistent across the app and the website on all platforms.
- Use a daily forecast rather than a weekly forecast for routine weather checks.
  适用条件：Routine checks; whole-week forecast reserved for trip planning
