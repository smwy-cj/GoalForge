# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Use Uber mainly to get from one place to another.
- Decide whether to take a ride by weighing the trip's duration and price.
  适用条件：Only when I cannot or do not want to drive there, or when no better transport option exists
- Walk to the destination instead of taking a ride when the ride would take about an hour and a half.
  适用条件：Even if the destination is far away
- Choose the cheapest ride option, except when traveling with more people.
  适用条件：Except when traveling with more people
- See nearby drivers on the map so I know whether drivers are available near me.
- Wait before booking when the price is too high.
- Avoid long booking waits and 'can't find a driver' failures after the app shows a driver nearby.
- Avoid canceling rides in order to protect my passenger rating.
- Rent a car for work travel or vacations when walkability, driving needs and trip duration make it worthwhile.
  适用条件：Preferred over rideshare when traveling for work or on vacation
- Prioritize arrival time over price on work rideshare trips because my employer covers the cost.
  适用条件：Employer reimburses the trip
- Take the bus for planned routes, including work trips covered by a free bus pass.
  适用条件：Work provides a free bus pass
- Avoid taking a bus in an area I am not familiar with.
- Take Uber instead of the bus when the bus trip would be long, require walking, or involve multiple transfers.
- Avoid using Uber Eats after a bad customer-service experience.
- Reach a human customer-support agent when I need an issue resolved.
  适用条件：Rather than relying on automated or chatbot responses for issue resolution
- Prefer a single destination over a multi-stop trip when using Uber.
- Keep multi-stop trips as quick as possible, avoiding long in-between stops.
- Call or message the driver in the app when the pickup point is hard to find or the area is busy.
  适用条件：House numbers hard to see, poor GPS, no place to pull over, many people around
- Let the driver know in the app when a multi-stop trip is taking longer than expected.
  适用条件：Multi-stop trip
- Give the driver a heads-up directly instead of changing the pickup location in the app.
  适用条件：When the pickup point needs to change
- Adjust the pickup location in the app easily even when it was set from the current location.
- Check the ride fare after the trip to split the charge with friends or submit it as a receipt for reimbursement.
