# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Use Uber to get from one place to another.
  适用条件：Typically for places the stakeholder could walk to
- Decide whether to take a ride mainly by the trip duration and the price, and walk instead when a ride would take too long (for example about an hour and a half).
  适用条件：Decision is about whether to take a ride at all
- Choose the cheapest ride option when booking, except when traveling with more people, where a larger four-seat car is chosen instead.
  适用条件：The exception applies when traveling with more people
- Wait instead of booking immediately when the quoted price is too high.
  适用条件：When the price is too high and the trip does not have to start now
- On work trips where the employer covers the cost, accept a higher price (for example 20 dollars more) to avoid a longer wait (for example 20 extra minutes).
  适用条件：Work travel where the employer covers the cost
- Avoid cancelling a booked ride, because cancellations damage the passenger rating.
  适用条件：Reason given: drivers see the passenger rating
- Be able to talk to a human customer-service agent when trying to resolve an issue.
  适用条件：For an issue that needs resolving rather than a specific question
- Prefer, most of the time, a single destination over multiple stops when taking an Uber.
  适用条件：Multi-stop is still used occasionally
- When a multi-stop trip is used, keep the in-between stops as quick as possible.
  适用条件：Only when a multi-stop trip is used
- Contact the driver through the app's call or messaging feature to coordinate the pickup when the pickup point is hard to find or the area is busy.
  适用条件：House numbers hard to see or poor GPS at the pickup point；Busy area with no good place to pull over or many people；Also used during multi-stop trips to tell the driver about delays
- Tell the driver directly about a pickup change instead of changing the pickup location in the app.
  适用条件：When the pickup location needs to change after booking
- Change the pickup location in the app with little effort after it has been set from the current location.
  适用条件：After the pickup point has been set from the current location
- View the ride fare after the trip to split the charge with friends or use it as a receipt for work reimbursement.
  适用条件：After the ride; for splitting with friends or work reimbursement
- Not use Uber Eats, having stopped after the bad experience with it.
  适用条件：Reason: badly estimated time and extraordinarily poor customer service
- Avoid taking the bus in areas the stakeholder is not familiar with.
  适用条件：When the area is unfamiliar
- Choose Uber over the bus when the bus trip would be long or would require walking or transferring.
  适用条件：When the bus route is long, requires walking, or needs several buses
- Rent a car for travel when walkability and the amount of driving make it sensible, including week-long trips with substantial travel, for more freedom.
  适用条件：Work or general travel; depends on how walkable the area is and how much driving is needed；Week-long trips with a decent amount of travel
