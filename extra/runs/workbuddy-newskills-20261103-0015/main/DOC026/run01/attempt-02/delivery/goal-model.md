# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Use Uber to get from one place to another, typically for destinations that are within walking distance.
- Choose whether to take a ride mainly by weighing trip duration and price against walking, driving or other transport, and walk instead when a ride would take about an hour and a half.
  适用条件：the trip could alternatively be walked or driven
- Choose the cheapest ride option, except when traveling with more people and a larger vehicle is needed.
  适用条件：traveling alone or in a small group
- Wait before booking when the quoted price is too high and the departure does not have to be immediate.
  适用条件：departure time is flexible
- Book a ride successfully when the app shows available drivers, without long scheduling delays or ending in a failure to find a driver.
  适用条件：the app is showing drivers nearby
- Avoid canceling a ride so that the passenger rating seen by drivers is not damaged.
  适用条件：drivers can see a passenger's rating (stakeholder's belief)
- Cancel a ride without having to start the ride first.
- Take the bus for planned routes such as the work commute.
  适用条件：work provides a free bus pass；the route is planned in advance
- Avoid taking the bus in areas I am not familiar with.
  适用条件：destination is in an unfamiliar area
- Prefer Uber over the bus when the bus trip would take a long time or require walking or multiple buses.
  适用条件：the bus trip would be long or need walking or transfers
- Prefer renting a car for trips that require a lot of driving or last several days.
  适用条件：depends on how walkable the area is and how much driving is needed；covers work travel and week-long vacations
- When taking a rideshare for work and the cost is covered, prioritize travel time over price.
  适用条件：work covers the cost of the ride
- Reach a human customer support agent who can actually resolve the issue.
  适用条件：a scripted or chatbot answer alone does not resolve the issue
- Get accurate delivery-time estimates for Uber Eats orders.
- Avoid using Uber Eats after bad delivery and support experiences.
- Prefer trips with a single destination over multi-stop trips.
- Keep any intermediate stop on a multi-stop trip as short as possible.
  适用条件：the trip involves an intermediate stop
- Call or message the driver in the app when the pickup spot is hard to find or the area is crowded.
  适用条件：house numbers are hard to see, GPS is poor, or the area is busy
- Give the driver a heads-up when an intermediate stop on a multi-stop trip takes longer than expected.
  适用条件：a multi-stop trip's intermediate stop runs long
- Be able to change the pickup point easily after a ride is booked when the ride starts from the current location.
  适用条件：booking starts from the current location instead of a typed address
- Notify the driver directly instead of changing the pickup point in the app when the pickup point needs to change.
  适用条件：the pickup point needs to change after booking
- Check the ride fare after the trip, to split the cost with friends or to use it as a receipt for work reimbursement.
  适用条件：shared ride with friends；work ride to be reimbursed
