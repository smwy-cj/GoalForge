# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Connect with colleagues and former classmates on LinkedIn to see what they are up to.
  适用条件：Networking with people he already knows is one of the main things he does on LinkedIn
- Market the business on LinkedIn to get exposure that is far better than Google Ads, Facebook or other channels deliver.
  适用条件：Marketing is central in his field of work；Cost is a consideration because the marketing resources charge an arm and a leg
- Recruit through LinkedIn to source a lot of people at a fairly low cost compared with competitors.
  适用条件：Compared with some of the other competitors
- Scan LinkedIn occasionally for other job openings and salary ranges to keep an eye on the market and stay motivated.
  适用条件：Personal use, described as poking around ('sometimes it's nice')
- Check LinkedIn for community events when he has the availability and bandwidth, because email alone leaves no chance of keeping up with everything.
  适用条件：Condition: only when availability and bandwidth allow；Email is described as a fire hose he cannot keep up with
- Signal to clients that he exists and holds credentials and degrees, so his presence lends credibility.
  适用条件：Stated as what LinkedIn is mostly for him, rather than professional development
- Let people check that he is who he says he is, not a scammer, with a picture that matches how he appears on Zoom or in person.
  适用条件：Described as another resource for people to check in
- Use LinkedIn's e-learning content for quick deep dives into a technology stack or a marketing topic, because its quality exceeds YouTube and other sources.
  适用条件：Expressed in the past tense as a preference ('I did like when they purchased Linda')
- Keep track of post impressions and marketing metrics, including for paid boosted recommendations.
  适用条件：Boosting is paid, with cost and expected boost stated up front；The process is described as really simple
- Post a marketing post for awareness on LinkedIn, for example about something interesting happening at a conference.
  适用条件：Posted as the company page, his personal profile, or both；Enriched with images, videos, backgrounds or emojis
- Schedule the post for 9 AM the next morning instead of the evening, so it lands when people first show up at the office and gets more impressions.
  适用条件：Evening posts get fewer impressions
- Avoid cold connection requests and typically only add people he already knows.
  适用条件：Soft habit: 'I don't often', 'I don't reach out too much'
- Search for a specific prospect's profile, for example a C-suite executive at a company they want to sell to, review the background for common threads, and then decide whether to add them.
  适用条件：Prospect is someone he is interested in learning more about；Connection decision stays tentative ('maybe I'll add them')
- Post recruiting ads in the Laravel community job board (Lara jobs) to get better candidates than LinkedIn recruiting posts produce.
  适用条件：The Laravel community is small；Applies to recruiting, where he says some tools are just better
- Keep his LinkedIn feed free of excessive ads and irrelevant targeted content.
  适用条件：Named as his biggest frustration
- Avoid interface clutter and added noise when he is on LinkedIn for a specific mission such as posting something to turn into impressions.
  适用条件：Clutter is described as information thrown in to make the interface busy
- Have more of content development automated on LinkedIn, so the company does not need as large a marketing team.
  适用条件：Framed as a wish ('would be great')；Existing new AI tools for post content are noted as new and interesting
- Post professional-looking content without being reviewed six times or going through the whole marketing funnel.
  适用条件：Design is at a premium and takes a while；Framed as a wish ('it'd be great')
