# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Connect with colleagues and former classmates on LinkedIn to see what they are up to.
- Run marketing on LinkedIn to obtain exposure that is far better than Google Ads, Facebook or other channels in the stakeholder's field.
  适用条件：in the stakeholder's field, where LinkedIn exposure is judged better than other channels
- Recruit through LinkedIn to source many candidates at a cost that is fairly low compared with competitors.
  适用条件：exception: in the small Laravel community, posting on Lara Jobs yields better candidates than LinkedIn
- Browse job openings for himself on LinkedIn and check salary ranges from time to time.
  适用条件：occasionally, when he feels like poking around
- Check community events posted on LinkedIn when he has the availability and bandwidth, since email alone makes it impossible to keep up.
  适用条件：only when he has availability/bandwidth；emails arrive in a firehose, so he cannot keep up otherwise
- Maintain a credible LinkedIn profile with credentials, degrees and a photo matching his real appearance, so that clients can confirm he is genuine and not a scammer.
  适用条件：for clients and people who receive his emails
- Use LinkedIn's easy, user-friendly posting tools, including paid recommendations, to put broad-based marketing in front of a targeted audience.
  适用条件：he already runs marketing on LinkedIn
- Prefer LinkedIn Learning for quick deep dives into a technology stack or a marketing practice, because its quality exceeds YouTube and similar resources.
  适用条件：when he wants a quick deep dive
- Publish marketing or awareness posts about events such as conferences, as the company and/or personal profile, with images, videos or other visual additions.
  适用条件：typically when something interesting happens at a conference；may post as the company profile, his personal profile, or both
- Schedule posts for about 9 AM the next morning when people first arrive at the office, instead of posting in the evening, to get more impressions.
  适用条件：posting in the evening is judged to get fewer impressions
- Gain interactions once a post is on the feed and, hopefully, eventually win some business.
  适用条件：outcome is hoped for, not guaranteed
- Before connecting with a new prospect, search the person's profile and review the background for common threads, and avoid cold calls or cold connection requests.
  适用条件：usually only connects with people he knows；example prospect: a C-level person at a company he wants to sell to
- Have LinkedIn provide more automated support for content development so that a smaller marketing team is needed.
  适用条件：suggestion strength ('would be great')
- Be able to post professional-looking content without six rounds of review or going through the whole marketing-funnel approval process.
  适用条件：design is at a premium and always takes a while
