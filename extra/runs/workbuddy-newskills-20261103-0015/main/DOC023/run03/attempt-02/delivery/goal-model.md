# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Increase the number of connections shown on my LinkedIn profile.
  适用条件：the count is what visitors see on his profile page
- Show the people who view my profile that I am active on LinkedIn.
- Obtain referrals from current employees of the company I am applying to, since internship and full-time applications are competitive and a referral helps my resume be seen by recruiters.
  适用条件：the target was referred to as 'my 3rd company'; the specific employer is not named in the transcript
- Search LinkedIn by entering both the target company name and the name of the university I currently attend, so that the results list people with the same background as me.
  适用条件：only when he wants to connect with people for a referral
- Connect only with people who are currently employed at the target company and who attended my university, skipping former employees and people who satisfy only one or none of these requirements.
  适用条件：former employees cannot refer him for the position；people who meet only one or none of the requirements are filtered out
- Open each candidate's profile to read their experience, and only send a connection request when that experience matches the position I am targeting.
  适用条件：connect only if their experience is exactly what he is looking for
- Send a connection note that introduces myself, says that I would like a referral for the position, and mentions that I was also a student at the same university, to raise the chance they agree to refer me.
  适用条件：LinkedIn limits the note to 200 characters for non-members；the note accompanies the connection request, before the invitation is accepted
- Purchase LinkedIn Premium so that I can access as many potential connections as I want.
  适用条件：chosen as the simplest option, but it costs a lot
- Adjust the search keywords — deleting one or two of them or making them more specific — so that LinkedIn returns people with a more precise background.
  适用条件：used as the alternative when results are unsatisfactory
