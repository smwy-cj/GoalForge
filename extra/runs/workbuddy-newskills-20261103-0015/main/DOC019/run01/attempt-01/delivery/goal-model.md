# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Plan finances long term and save money for retirement.
  适用条件：首要目标（primary goal）
- Become more financially literate while investing, including understanding how markets work.
  适用条件：次要目标（secondary goal）
- Understand new algorithms that can be used to generate profits.
  适用条件：次要目标（secondary goal）；转写稿 'gardener profits' 应按 generate profits 理解
- Become algorithmically literate in finance to help in getting jobs.
  适用条件：第三目标（tertiary goal）
- Integrate the user's own bank account seamlessly at the start of onboarding.
  适用条件：银行账户绑定发生在开户最初阶段；受访者称其为是否使用某应用的决定因素
- Convert cash from the bank into the desired cryptocurrency and back into cash inside the app.
  适用条件：当时在印度使用 Coinbase；法币与加密资产双向转换
- Be able to set up rules for trading.
  适用条件：理想交易体验的一部分
- Have a separate interface for financial news that shows what has happened in the market and how it can affect individual stocks.
  适用条件：参照 Coinbase：每条标题标注对具体股票/币种的正负面并配图标；新闻是受访者主要信息来源
- See how a particular stock is moving at that moment.
  适用条件：'trades easily' 的第一项含义
- Place a trade instantly with the least amount of time and effort.
  适用条件：'trades easily' 的第二项含义；受访者以最少时间和最少精力评价 Binance
- Keep a single-page portfolio dashboard that summarizes the day's gains and losses.
  适用条件：受访者每天使用该视图；被称为最喜欢的功能之一
- Provide more guidance on advanced trading algorithms once the user is already using the app.
  适用条件：用户已进入应用并开始使用之后；'I am not sure of this' 指对当时 Academy 内容的记忆不确定
- Provide comprehensive academy coverage of futures and options trading.
  适用条件：Academy 课程范围；受访者不确定现在是否已经补充（'I don't know if they have done it now'）
- Offer mutual fund investing in addition to individual stocks.
  适用条件：可行性未知（'I'm not sure how feasible it is'）；预期更安全、可投入更大金额、信任度更高
- Make onboarding as easy as possible.
  适用条件：开户流程；即便最易用的应用也仍显得复杂
- Add voice-based orders or voice-based summarization so the user does not have to open the app repeatedly to check or ask.
  适用条件：'if it is possible' 表示可行性未定；语音下单与语音摘要为两种替代形式（or）

## 目标之间的联系

下列联系不表示充分精化；待确认及已否定记录保留各自状态。

- 有依据的联系：Integrate the user's own bank account seamlessly at the start of onboarding. —[范围具体化]→ Make onboarding as easy as possible.
  依据：G005 是开户最初环节的具体目标，G015 是开户整体尽可能简单的目标；T052 明确说复杂点就在开户的银行账户部分，T054 要求整个开户尽可能简单，故具体环节指向一般目标。
  条件／假设：银行账户绑定属于开户流程的初始环节
- 有依据的联系：Convert cash from the bank into the desired cryptocurrency and back into cash inside the app. —[有助于]→ Place a trade instantly with the least amount of time and effort.
  依据：T018 说明无法把银行的钱换成本想投资的币，连第一步投资都做不到；法币通路对即时下单是有帮助的前提，但不保证下单本身的体验。
  条件／假设：资金需先能进入交易账户
- 有依据的联系：Provide more guidance on advanced trading algorithms once the user is already using the app. —[实现方式]→ Understand new algorithms that can be used to generate profits.
  依据：T044 把'更多高级算法指引'作为获得算法理解的途径，T024 的目标是理解可用于获利的新算法；在应用内提供指引服务于该学习目的，但不保证学懂全部算法。
- 有依据的联系：Provide comprehensive academy coverage of futures and options trading. —[实现方式]→ Become more financially literate while investing, including understanding how markets work.
  依据：T044 的期货与期权课程属于 Academy 学习内容，服务于 T024 的金融素养目标；课程只是素养的一个来源，不覆盖全部素养要求。
- 待确认联系：Integrate the user's own bank account seamlessly at the start of onboarding. —[有助于]→ Convert cash from the bank into the desired cryptocurrency and back into cash inside the app.
  依据：T018 把'绑定银行账户的顺畅接口'与'法币与加密资产转换的支付接口'并列提出，绑定账户可能帮助资金转换；但原文未说明两者机制上的依赖，故标 proposed。
  条件／假设：银行账户绑定是法币出入金的技术前提
