# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Save up money through long-term financial planning so that funds are available after retirement
  适用条件：原文明确为首要目标（primary goal）；长期（long term）规划，不是短期投机
- Become more financially literate, including understanding how markets work
  适用条件：原文列为次要目标（secondary goal）；是在使用这些应用的过程中提升（in the process）
- Understand new algorithms that can be used to generate profits
  适用条件：与金融素养同属次要目标的一部分；关注对象是公司所用算法与活跃交易者的做法（T044）
- Become algorithmically literate in finance in order to help get jobs
  适用条件：原文列为第三层目标（tertiary goal）；目的是求职助力，不是直接用于获利
- Get in-app guidance on advanced trading algorithms beyond the basic ones
  适用条件：受访者对学院目前是否仍只有基础算法不确定（I am not sure of this）；指导内容指向公司所用算法与活跃交易者的做法；是在进入应用并使用之后再获得的指导（Once I am into the app）
- Have comprehensive Academy content on futures and options trading
  适用条件：原文说明当时只是一带而过（in a very running pattern）；受访者不确定现在是否已补上（I don't know if they have done it now）；作为对学院内容的补充建议提出（would be good addition）
- Have a separate interface in the app for financial news that explains what happened in the market and can affect different stocks
  适用条件：属于他描述的理想体验（ideal experience）；与交易界面分开（separate interface）；0 Dha 目前没有这一板块（T038）
- See financial news as a queue of headlines, each marked positive or negative (green or red) for the particular stock or cryptocurrency
  适用条件：原文是对 Coinbase 该板块的描述，不是对 0 Dha 的直接要求；结合 T028 希望在应用内获得财经新闻、T037 追问 0 Dha 是否有该板块推导
- Rely on news from various external sources (newspapers, online articles, Youtube channels) when deciding what to invest in
  适用条件：约占其信息来源的 99%；除新闻外还有当日发生的事情；目前以应用外的新闻门户补齐（T036），0 Dha 内没有新闻板块
- Integrate a bank account with the trading app seamlessly, with onboarding made as easy as possible
  适用条件：是否使用某个应用的决定因素（deciding factor of using or not using an app）；应用只需核验信息正确即可完成绑定；开户/onboarding 是他认为唯一复杂的部分；即便 0 Dha 已是最简单，他仍希望尽可能更简单
- Be able to convert between bank cash and cryptocurrency inside the app through a payment interface
  适用条件：在 Coinbase 上无法完成（that is one thing I was not able to do）；包含 crypto 换现金与现金换 crypto 两个方向
- Make a trade instantly and with the least amount of time and effort
  适用条件：即便加密货币交易存在法律法规限制，也要能顺畅完成（尤其作为印度客户）；行情连续变动，需要快速决策
- Understand how a particular stock is moving at that point of time
  适用条件：'trades easily' 定义的第一部分；用于下单前判断
- Be able to set up rules for trading
  适用条件：与即时下单并列（or）的另一种交易方式；属于理想体验的一部分
- Realign (rebalance) investments about every two weeks to a month based on review
  适用条件：具体决策为在哪里卖出、在哪里加仓（Where do I need to sell? Where do I need to buy more）；加密资产约每两周查看一次
- Track every day how his investments and the market have moved, including which stocks are performing well or not
  适用条件：每天收盘后查看（every day at the end of the day）；股票应用约每天打开一次
- See the portfolio dashboard giving, in one page, the day's picture: what went up, what went down and the net profit or loss
  适用条件：最常使用的工具（most frequently used tool）；投资组合页还可查看投资表现与已下订单（T032）
- Keep a watchlist of interesting stocks and keep a tab on them
  适用条件：使用频率不高但会持续留意（I don't use it a lot, but I do keep a tab）；watchlist 是他登录后看到的第一个仪表盘内容（T032）
- Review the orders already placed
  适用条件：在投资组合页内查看（T032）；下单本身由 G012 承接，此处仅指查看已下订单
- Decide on a holding by considering its market-cap segment (small / mid / large cap), the attached risk and liquidity, and its performance over the past about 3 to 6 months
  适用条件：流动性指更容易买入或卖出；小市值看近 3 个月、大市值看近 6 个月表现；适用于他做投资决策的场景（T021 追问决策过程）
- Exit a large cap holding that has not performed well over about the past 6 months, because it indicates investors are pulling out
  适用条件：触发条件为大市值股近 6 个月表现不佳；理由是有投资者在撤出、可能掌握他所没有的信息
- Sell a holding when he feels the profit from that trade has been maximized
  适用条件：以'觉得利润已最大化'为主观判断；与按表现撤出（G021）属不同触发条件
- Stay invested in a small cap stock even when it has gone down recently, if he has a positive outlook on it
  适用条件：例外/容忍条件：对按表现撤出的例外；理由是他认为下跌是因为他人尚未看到其潜力
- Focus on long-term investments rather than intraday trading
  适用条件：指认真交易阶段（最初一个月为试水）；偏好表述（when I say trading, I mean ...）
- Avoid exposure to highly volatile cryptocurrencies and to scam coins
  适用条件：相对于股票与印度股市而言；规避对象为高波动与诈骗币两类
- Keep cryptocurrency investment to blue chip coins (bitcoin, ethereum, polygon) as relatively safe investments
  适用条件：会持续买入（continually invest）；理由是被视为相对安全的投资
- Keep the amount invested in stocks much higher than the amount invested in cryptocurrencies
  适用条件：比较对象是自己的股票投入与加密投入；强度为 much, much higher
- Have the option of investing in mutual funds in the app, apart from just stocks
  适用条件：认为更安全，可投入更大金额且更信任；可行性不确定（I'm not sure how feasible it is）；也不确定这是否是应用方的目标（I'm not sure if that is their goal or not）
- Place orders by voice (voice based orders)
  适用条件：可行性不确定（if it is possible / I'm not sure）；作为最想增加的一项功能被提出（an addition）
- Get voice based summarization
  适用条件：可行性不确定；与语音下单以 or 并列的替代方案
- Avoid having to go into the app all the time just to check whatever he wants to ask
  适用条件：避免型诉求（reduce the pain）；语音功能（G029/G030）所服务的目的
- Stay with the current app once set up and avoid switching to a new app, because setting up the system is time taking
  适用条件：例外：新应用有他觉得离不开、非常惊艳的功能时可能转换；转换意愿自评为 1-2（1 为最不倾向）
- Use a trading app that is user friendly and easy to use
  适用条件：选择加密货币应用时明确给出的理由（Binance 因 user friendly 被选中）；0 Dha 被评价为最简单的一个，但仍被认为复杂（T054）
