# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Ensure no one else can access his accounts on any social media platform.
  适用条件：Strong authentication (e.g., Signal's backup-code 2FA) is what earns his trust
- Keep his tweets and posts (Instagram, Reddit) from mysteriously disappearing — they should remain unless and until he deletes or archives them.
- Avoid frequent platform outages — sporadic outages are acceptable, but outages that are too regular would drive him away from the platform.
  适用条件：Depends on the network he is on
- Prevent people he does not know from seeing through his texts or conversations with friends.
- Have his data encrypted on the social media platforms he uses.
- Have social media advertising use basic human judgment and the right balance instead of repeatedly showing him the same ads triggered by a single mention.
  适用条件：Exception: ads are occasionally helpful when he actually wants to buy something (rare)；Otherwise he gets frustrated, exits the app, and goes somewhere without ads
- Keep his anonymity intact while using Reddit.
- Use Reddit to find answers to the questions and issues he is stuck on, in particular situations such as finding recommendations.
  适用条件：Very niche situations (e.g., experience with a specific company's interview) may yield no answers on Reddit
- Get to know the news and things happening back in India while he is in the US, despite Twitter being set to his US location.
  适用条件：Obstacle: Twitter is set to his location in the US
- Stay connected to the world so that he never remains unaware of something big that has happened.
- Know what is happening in his friend circle, with friends back in India, and around him locally (campus, class, classmates) through social networking apps.
- Stay connected to his friends in real time (e.g., via Snapchat instant photo and video sharing).
- Use Twitter as his go-to news app to follow what is happening around the world via trending hashtags.
  适用条件：Also values tech news, memes, and easily following trends on Twitter
- Find the right content he is looking for when using social media.
- Be shown an assortment of different kinds of content rather than one repeated stream (e.g., Instagram repeatedly showing food videos).
  适用条件：If he actually wanted one stream of content he would go to that specific channel or group
- Have new posts load in real time without lag so he does not have to scroll back up to refresh and risk missing posts.
  适用条件：Depends on the network he is on
- Keep track of all his social media account credentials and 2FA backup codes in a digital vault (currently Google Password Manager), since losing it would mean losing most of his account details.
  适用条件：Currently uses Google Authenticator / Google Password Manager, which he finds reliable with autofill
- Continue to be able to filter out what is authentic and what is not on social media so that he is never misinformed.
- Have one common, self-designed platform that aggregates content from all his favorite social media apps in one place so he does not waste time switching between apps.
  适用条件：He would add his chosen social media apps to the platform and the platform would show content from the added apps
