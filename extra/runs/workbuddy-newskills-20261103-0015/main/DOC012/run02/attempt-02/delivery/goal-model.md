# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Submit the assignments I have to do for my course through the course's web app (Canvas or Gradescope).
  适用条件：the course sets up the Canvas or Gradescope pages where the assignments are accessible
- Submit each assignment before its due date (in time).
- Before submitting an assignment, open its description and fetch the information needed to do the assignment.
  适用条件：before submitting
- Submit a PDF report by uploading the file with the file uploader in Canvas.
  适用条件：when the file to submit is a PDF report
- Submit a code repository by using Gradescope's feature to fetch my latest commit from GitHub and submit my codebase.
  适用条件：when the assignment is a code repository
- Have coding projects auto-graded by the app, because that is very convenient for coding projects.
  适用条件：for coding projects
- Know my grade immediately after submitting a code assignment, while taking it as not the final grade.
  适用条件：after a code submission is auto-graded；the immediate grade is not the final grade
- See my grade for the assignment on the assignment page after it has been graded.
  适用条件：after the assignment is graded
- Get feedback in the form of a document with comments after the assignment is graded, so I can see in detail where I can improve.
  适用条件：after the assignment is graded；feedback comes from the professor or TAs
- See the feedback comments while viewing my submitted assignment in the document viewer.
  适用条件：in the document viewer reached from the assignment page
- Have feedback comments attached to the specific place in the assignment they refer to, instead of being inserted only in a global part.
  适用条件：when professors or TAs add comments in the app
- Submit a regrade request in Gradescope when I think a comment is wrong or not applicable to my assignment and I should not lose a point there.
  适用条件：only in Gradescope
- Have a regrade-request feature in Canvas too, so I do not have to fall back on email or other communication tools to contest a grade.
  适用条件：in Canvas, which currently has no such feature
- Contact the professor about a grading problem through a convenient messaging tool such as a Slack channel or DM, rather than email.
  适用条件：depends on whether the professor has set up such a channel
- See a detailed per-part grading breakdown (subgrades) through the rubric feature in Canvas.
  适用条件：in Canvas, which Gradescope does not have
- See a real-time estimated grade for my previously submitted and graded assignments in Canvas.
  适用条件：in Canvas；for assignments already submitted and graded
- Decide how much time to spend on a course using the estimated-grade / indirect feedback information.
- Decide whether to try extra credit to boost my grade using the estimated-grade / indirect feedback information.
- Reach the assignment page or grading page by going through the app homepage/dashboard and selecting the course.
  适用条件：in Canvas or Gradescope
- See the list of assignments I have previously submitted and have not yet submitted, and choose one to look inside.
  适用条件：on the assignment page or grading page
- Use another app when one app does not have enough features, so the needed feature is supported.
  适用条件：when one of the apps lacks the needed feature
- When a professor publishes the assignment on a website, get all the details from that website and then submit through the linked app such as Gradescope.
  适用条件：depends on the professor's preference
- Know how much time remains before an assignment is due through the app.
  适用条件：in Gradescope
- Pick up the assignment without having to go through the grade page.
  适用条件：in Canvas, where the assignment is sometimes the same as the grade page
- Review the transcripts of the recorded interview before submitting the individual assignments.
- Have the interview session recorded and get it transcribed for the assignment, or failing that hand over the recording.
- Fill the time I spend biking, driving, cooking and doing other activities with video or music so that I am entertained.
  适用条件：mainly on the YouTube phone app
- Be entertained while exercising (running or on the exercise bike), which can be monotonous.
  适用条件：at the gym, on the phone
- Watch a video at home while doing other things such as cooking.
  适用条件：at home, while doing other activities
- Not disturb other people at the gym (by using headphones).
  适用条件：at the gym
- Listen to music and watch videos on YouTube without ads.
  适用条件：through the paid Google plan that includes YouTube Music and YouTube Premium, held for a few years
- Use YouTube as my main app for both watching videos and music.
  适用条件：also uses Netflix or Hulu at his parents' place, but almost always uses YouTube for videos
- Browse the YouTube recommendations screen to find a video that appeals to me.
  适用条件：goes through roughly 5 or 10 videos on that screen
- Keep videos saved in a Watch later list for later viewing.
- Put a video I want to watch but not right now into my Watch later list.
- Continue watching by clicking the recommendations shown for the current video.
  适用条件：after the first video；only if he wants to watch that recommendation
- Watch videos that other people recommend to me, especially long-form ones.
- Choose content that fits how I feel at the moment (demanding content such as hard math only when I am well rested).
- Pick videos about topics I want to learn more about (games, geography, history).
- Replay music I listened to recently from the app's history menu.
  适用条件：on the phone app
- Search for the specific music or video I want to watch.
- Choose a video whose length fills the time I have available (exactly the remaining bike time, otherwise a 10 to 30 minute video as a safe bet).
- Watch longer videos rather than shorts when I am on YouTube.
  适用条件：because he only opens YouTube when he has a bunch of time to fill
- Leave a video after a few minutes if it turns out not to be good.
- Find something to watch when the recommendations do not surface anything I want, by going to Watch later or searching for something I am interested in.
- Click off a video the app gives me that I do not want.
- Read the comments for a while to see whether a video gets better.
- Have smarter users in YouTube comments (my biggest wish for YouTube), because commenters are often pretty dumb.
  适用条件：states he does not leave comments very often, so it would not affect him directly
- Maybe require some sort of basic test (an IQ-style test or even just any basic test) before people are allowed to comment.
  适用条件：suggested as a way to make the app more optimal; also mentions a sobriety/breathalyser test in a joking tone
- Avoid story-like content that depends on having watched the previous episode and requires sustained attention.
  适用条件：movies or TV shows are pretty long and require watching over and over and paying attention
- Have a bunch of stuff I can watch out of order, in whatever order, or skip, which is better for my attention span.
- Watch more short-form, nonfiction content.
- Get basically an endless stream of heavily algorithmically curated videos.
- Have the quantity and the diversity of videos that I want available on the platform.
- Find a new thing to watch easily.
- Have recommendations that reflect my long-standing preferences and watch history.
  适用条件：about ten years of Google account history, and roughly 17 years of watching YouTube
- Stay on YouTube instead of switching to another streaming platform.
- Download videos in advance when I have good Wi-Fi beforehand, so I have them for a long trip / driving.
  适用条件：before a long trip with good Wi-Fi；requires the advanced version of the app
- Have downloading videos be easy in the app.
  适用条件：if you have the advanced version of the app
- Have the app's content categories divided the way I would subdivide my own interests (a few music genres, Breaking Bad stuff, board games, math things, video game stuff), so it is easier for me to watch.
  适用条件：uses the phone app, basically never the YouTube web app for music
