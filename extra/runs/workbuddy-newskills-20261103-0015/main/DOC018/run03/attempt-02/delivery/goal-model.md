# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Export course material directly from the school portal to a chosen cloud destination such as Google Drive, so it reaches the iPad with fewer steps and without being downloaded to the computer first.
  适用条件：the destination is left open: Google Drive or a Kindle account；stated as the highest-priority feature
- Open both PDFs and ebooks for school reading in one single application instead of across scattered places.
  适用条件：at least for school material；the application would be used primarily on the iPad
- Save valuable course PDFs into a personal student library so they stay accessible after the course material is no longer available.
  适用条件：after the semester ends the course material is withdrawn and access has to be requested again
- Filter and search previously read material by when it was read, by month or year.
  适用条件：recall usually starts from the term and year, e.g. 'spring 2023', not from the title or ISBN
- Show reading progress for the current chapter as a percentage.
  适用条件：the app already shows overall book percentage and remaining time for the chapter；per-chapter view is meant to make long chapters less daunting
- Have the existing Kindle app manage PDFs as well.
  适用条件：PDFs currently cannot be ported to Kindle as far as the stakeholder knows；offered as the easier option when asked whether the consolidated app should be standalone or reuse an existing app
- Have the school provide its own online reading application so that articles sent by professors are available directly on the iPad.
  适用条件：without downloading and uploading material first
- Have the reading application included without a separate payment by students.
  适用条件：asked whether he would pay for that application
