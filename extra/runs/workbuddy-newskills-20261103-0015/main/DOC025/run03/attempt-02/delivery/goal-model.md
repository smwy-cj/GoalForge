# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Explore job opportunities on various job boards despite the market not being good.
  适用条件：job market is not that good now
- Expand professional network on LinkedIn.
- Stay updated with the industry through LinkedIn.
- Explore job opportunities on LinkedIn, including browsing job postings and job recommendations received from the app.
- Find specific jobs on LinkedIn by searching keywords (e.g., software engineer), location, and filters such as date posted and experience level.
- Apply to job opportunities on LinkedIn using the easy apply feature that automatically reuses the LinkedIn profile, saving application time.
  适用条件：easy apply 流程中有时会被要求补充上传简历或回答特定问题
- See posts from their network in the LinkedIn news feed, including updates from connections, followed companies, suggested posts, and posts from joined groups.
- Engage with posts that interest them by liking, commenting, or sharing.
- Connect with someone they already know on LinkedIn by searching their name and sending a connection request with a personalized note.
- Join LinkedIn groups that look active and relevant, discovered through suggested groups based on their profile and connections.
- Connect with people within the same LinkedIn group.
- Receive job alert notifications with recommended job postings for saved job search filters, via the app or email based on settings.
  适用条件：通知渠道按设置可为应用内通知或邮件
- Manage the frequency (daily or weekly) and notification type of job alerts for each saved job filter.
- Access the list of saved jobs under My Jobs, alongside in-progress, applied, and archived categories.
- Edit personal profile sections (about, education, position) on LinkedIn and save the changes.
- Upload resume to LinkedIn.
- Update LinkedIn profile status to indicate being open to work, selecting finding a new job and setting preferences.
- Filter job search results by specific skills or technologies rather than just job titles or locations.
  适用条件：LinkedIn 目前不提供该细粒度选项（原文：But Linkedin doesn't provide these granular options），此为期望的改进能力
- Look for jobs that mention specific software or programming languages.
- Connect with people outside the LinkedIn network, such as those with strict privacy settings, via personalized connection requests.
  适用条件：请求有时无回应或被忽略；LinkedIn 限制与网络外人员的直接连接
