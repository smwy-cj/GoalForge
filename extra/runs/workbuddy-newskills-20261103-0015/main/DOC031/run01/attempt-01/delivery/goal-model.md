# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Achieve instant communication with anyone within the university, looking the person up by name without saving their number or email, from either phone or laptop.
  适用条件：Contact is initiated by looking the person up by name, without pre-saved contact details；Accessible from both phone and laptop
- Reach a wide audience immediately through the channel feature.
  适用条件：Faster than reaching the same audience by email
- Have a channel's earlier history available at the moment of joining, including information posted before joining.
  适用条件：Applies when joining a channel
- Stay aware of what people are talking about and sharing, on a particular subject or in general, and of what is happening in the department, the program and around campus.
- Share a document instantly with a teammate while collaborating, attaching any type of file from local folders.
  适用条件：While collaborating with teammates in direct messaging
- Locate a specific past message by entering keywords in the search feature, for example something a professor said.
- Have the call feature be more accessible, with fewer windows to navigate to reach it, especially in a group setting.
- Send an email to a person from Slack through a quick email feature that immediately navigates to a standard email app such as Outlook or Gmail.
  适用条件：In situations where an email is expected, e.g. formal communication with teaching assistants or professors
- Consider improving navigation within Slack when the browser window is shrunk to sit side by side with other open windows.
  适用条件：Works well on a phone and on a 14-inch laptop at full size; the difficulty appears with a shrunk browser window；Raised as an area of improvement that could be considered
- Continue using Slack for communication within the university rather than switching to another application.
  适用条件：Because Slack provides options to collaborate and to make a call；Because Slack is regarded as the fastest way to communicate with people within the university
