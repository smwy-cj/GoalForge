# 目标模型

版本：v1。人工待审。

- Reach any member of the university almost immediately by looking them up by name or surname, without saving their contact details, from either phone or laptop.
  适用条件：The person is a member of the university community；Only a name or surname is known
- Reach a wide audience immediately by posting in a channel, instead of using a slower channel such as email.
- Have a channel's history immediately available at the fingertips when entering it, including content posted before joining.
  适用条件：On joining a channel
- Stay aware of what people are discussing and sharing in the department, in the program, and around campus.
- Share documents instantly with teammates during collaboration, with any file type supported.
  适用条件：Collaborating with teammates in a direct message
- Make the audio and video call feature easier to reach with fewer navigation steps, especially for calls in a group setting.
  适用条件：Compared with Microsoft Teams
- Get a quick handoff from Slack to a standard email app (Outlook, Gmail or similar) to email a person when an email is expected, such as formal communication with teaching assistants or professors.
  适用条件：Formal communication；With teaching assistants or professors
- Keep using Slack as the main tool for communicating with people in the university instead of switching to another application.
  适用条件：Even though calling is less convenient than in Teams
- Consider improving how easily Slack can be navigated when the browser window is narrowed for side-by-side use with several windows open.
  适用条件：Three windows open side by side；Shrunk browser window on a large screen
- Locate the specific past message being looked for when a keyword search returns far more results than can be sifted through.
  适用条件：Search result set much larger than the target；Exact target not yet known
