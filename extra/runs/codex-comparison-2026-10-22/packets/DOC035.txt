## PAPER summarized goals
g0 Display headline articles of top stories on a homepage.
g1 Deliver timely news articles on fast-moving topics and personal interests.
g2 Provide short and comprehensive articles covering every aspect of events.
g3 Send non-spammy and useful notifications about news articles.
g4 Ensure seamless navigation through news articles and sections.
g5 Enhance the smoothness of the news reading experience.
g6 Show headlines in time-constrained situations.
g7 Include the headline and an image in notifications.
g8 Include a snippet of the preface in notifications.
g9 Deliver breaking news alerts with high priority.
g10 Provide personalized news recommendations based on user behavior and interests.
g11 Deliver news notifications to users based on their interests.
g12 Allow users to share news articles by copying the link or through chat applications.
g13 Ensure users receive relevant news information efficiently.
g14 Provide articles tailored to user interests.
g15 Prioritize articles based on user interests and relevance.
g16 Offer concise versions of articles for quick reading.
g17 Allow users to access and organize articles by topic for later reading.
g18 Provide articles of varying lengths to match user preferences.
g19 Enable search functionality for events or topics.
g20 Remove ads from news pages.
g21 Allow users to disable topics they are not interested in.
g22 Ensure breaking news is displayed for disabled topics, without detailed articles or analysis.
g23 Provide news articles about technology developments, including new devices and AI models.
g24 Provide news articles about company-related information and activities, such as layoffs in the technology sector.
g25 Provide sports news related to Formula 1.
g26 Provide sports news related to cricket.
g27 Provide news content about Indian and American politics.
g28 Provide sources and citations to verify the credibility of information.
g29 Evaluate sources based on the speed of publication after events.
g30 Provide all relevant information quickly.
g31 Retrieve news articles from various sources to present different perspectives.
g32 Enable comparison of stances of different news sources on specific issues.
g33 Curate notifications to prioritize important updates and prevent missing them.
g34 Deliver notifications based on user behavior to enhance relevance and usefulness.
g35 Enable users to define their own topic buckets to organize news articles.
g36 Allow users to add multiple news articles to a topic bucket.
g37 Provide information and recommendations based on user's interests and habits.
g38 Ensure the interface is intuitive and user-friendly to facilitate easy navigation and actions.
g39 Ensure user data is not stored beyond what is necessary for providing recommendations.
g40 Prevent companies from using user data to promote their own agendas or propaganda.
