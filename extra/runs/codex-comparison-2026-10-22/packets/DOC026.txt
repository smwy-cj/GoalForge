## PAPER summarized goals
g0 Enable efficient and cost-effective transportation to a destination.
g1 Provide suggestions for destinations based on travel history.
g2 Offer convenient transportation options for unfamiliar areas.
g3 Deliver transportation solutions that minimize wait times.
g4 Facilitate transportation decisions based on area familiarity.
g5 Provide flexibility and freedom for extended travel.
g6 Simplify complex transportation routes.
g7 Consider time and cost efficiency in deciding transportation options.
g8 Provide alternative transportation options with comparisons of estimated fares, travel time, and capacity.
g9 Display an estimated arrival time for each transportation option.
g10 Optimize the process of booking a ride for immediate or scheduled travel.
g11 Ensure a seamless and efficient experience for booking and managing multi-stop rides.
g12 Enable users to easily update their pickup location during the booking process.
g13 Provide autocomplete suggestions for inputted addresses.
g14 Allow users to reach and interact with human customer support representatives.
g15 Inform users when they are interacting with a chat bot versus a human.
g16 Automatically apply promotional deals to bookings.
g17 Show real-time location of available vehicles and drivers on a map.
g18 Display accurate driver availability status.
g19 Enable direct communication between users and drivers for pickup details or location changes.
g20 Allow users to delay travel if the current price is deemed too high.
g21 Notify the user if no drivers are available after a booking attempt.
g22 Inform the user about the impact of cancellations on their rating.
g23 Prevent passenger rating from being negatively impacted by ride cancellations.
g24 Facilitate users in notifying drivers about delays during multi-stop trips.
g25 Simplify the process for service cancellation.
g26 Accurately estimate the time needed to complete services.
g27 Provide effective customer service for resolving issues.
g28 Allow users to rate the driver after a ride.
g29 Allow users to tip the driver after a ride.
g30 Provide users with a breakdown of ride costs for expense splitting.
g31 Enable users to access ride details for reimbursement purposes.
