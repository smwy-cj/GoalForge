## PAPER summarized goals
g0 Receive timely information on important international events.
g1 Receive timely information on new job postings.
g2 View a list of relevant job postings upon accessing the main page.
g3 Scroll through a list to locate intended job postings.
g4 Receive personalized recommendations based on user preferences and interactions.
g5 Provide tailored updates and information according to user preferences.
g6 Deduce user preferences from their browsing history.
g7 Prevent flooding of feeds with irrelevant content.
g8 Access a daily ranking of trending news.
g9 Select specific news stories from the ranked list.
g10 Search for information using a search bar.
g11 Enable users to search for information based on a specific region.
g12 Provide recommendations for job listings using artificial intelligence.
g13 Filter job postings based on region to provide relevant results.
g14 Indicate if a company has a high benchmark to pass the interview.
g15 Provide information on the number of people entering the hiring process at a company.
g16 Provide feedback from recent interviewees of a company.
g17 Provide a feature to separate and filter entertainment content from useful content.
g18 Deliver entertainment content in the form of jokes, photos, and text.
g19 Ensure user data is not leaked.
g20 Prevent unauthorized sharing of user data.
g21 Minimize damage caused by data leaks.
g22 Provide users with recourse in case of data misuse or leaks.
