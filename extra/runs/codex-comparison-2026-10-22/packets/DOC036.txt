## PAPER summarized goals
g0 Identify and display vendors showcasing specific products of interest on social media.
g1 Provide contact information for reaching out to vendors featured on social media.
g2 Facilitate discovering and contacting vendors through showcased products online.
g3 Show vendor accounts when a product or service is searched.
g4 Display a variety of products from one vendor in a visually browsable manner.
g5 Display the price of products clearly.
g6 Enable communication with vendors for purchasing decisions.
g7 Provide flexibility in making choices without prior planning.
g8 Enable purchasing decisions based on social influences and trends.
g9 Facilitate convenience in purchasing products in a new location.
g10 Include product prices to facilitate purchasing decisions.
g11 Identify and locate vendors based on user-specified location criteria.
g12 Allow refinement of search results with the ability to add location details.
g13 Save and allow access to past search results for future reference.
g14 Identify reputable vendors through analysis of engagement patterns over time.
g15 Allow users to communicate with vendors through various channels including an integrated chat and messaging apps.
g16 Provide users with direct access to vendor contact and business information.
g17 Streamline the payment process by making vendor payment details easily obtainable.
g18 Facilitate delivery coordination by confirming delivery address and providing delivery updates to users.
g19 Enable users to securely verify accounts to prevent fraudulent activity.
g20 Detect potentially malicious accounts through analysis of engagement metrics and activity.
g21 Help users decide which accounts to trust and purchase from to avoid scams.
g22 Enable users to search for and view account and contact details.
g23 Allow users to browse and use the application without signing in.
g24 Allow users to search for and connect with other users by name.
g25 Enable users to share personal photos and activities with others.
g26 Provide options for users to control who can view their online status.
g27 Allow the user to disable or limit access when not in active use.
g28 Enable users to follow others and view their content and activities.
g29 Minimize response time by ensuring low latency for active accounts.
g30 Provide visibility into user engagement through metrics like comments and likes.
g31 Facilitate browsing of user reviews and comments to help in decision-making.
g32 Provide an infinite scrolling feature for continuous content updates.
g33 Facilitate user engagement through continuous and engaging content features.
g34 Provide privacy controls to restrict account visibility to approved users.
g35 Facilitate reconnection with friends using alternative contact methods.
g36 Allow users to follow certain accounts without requiring mutual interaction.
g37 Enable users to follow other users, groups, or hashtags.
g38 Allow users to view updates or content from people, groups, or hashtags they follow in a continuous stream.
g39 Ensure users can view content from followed accounts or hashtags privately.
g40 Enable users to browse anonymously.
g41 Allow users to post anonymously.
